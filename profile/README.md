## 🚀 Overview
OpenCut for Windows is a free, open-source video editor built for creators who want to edit footage quickly without sending files to the cloud. It gives you a clean, timeline-based workspace where you can trim clips, rearrange scenes, add text and audio, and export a finished video straight from your PC. As an open-source project developed openly on GitHub, OpenCut is transparent about how it works and free to use, with a design that keeps your media processing local. Whether you are putting together short social clips or longer projects, the OpenCut download gives you a lightweight editor that runs on everyday Windows hardware.

## ✨ Features
- **Timeline editing** — Cut, split, trim, and reorder clips on a straightforward multi-track timeline built for fast everyday editing.
- **Privacy-first, local workflow** — Your footage is handled on your own PC, so you stay in control of your files instead of uploading them to a server.
- **Free and open-source** — The full OpenCut app is free to use and openly developed on GitHub, so anyone can inspect, learn from, or contribute to the project.
- **Text, audio, and effects** — Add titles and captions, layer in music or voiceover, and apply basic adjustments to shape the look of your video.
- **Fast export** — Render your project to a shareable video file directly on your Windows PC when your edit is ready.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open OpenCut and create a new project.
2. Import your video, image, and audio files into the media panel.
3. Drag your clips onto the timeline and trim or split them where you want cuts.
4. Add text, adjust audio, and arrange your scenes into the final sequence.
5. Export the finished video to a file on your PC and share it.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is OpenCut free?**
Yes. OpenCut is a free, open-source video editor. The OpenCut app is free to download and use for Windows, and its source code is available on GitHub.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
OpenCut is designed to run on modern 64-bit versions of Windows, including Windows 10 and Windows 11. Installing the latest stable release is recommended for the best experience.

**Is my data secure?**
OpenCut is built around a privacy-first approach: your video editing happens locally on your own PC, so you decide where your files live rather than handing them to a remote service.
